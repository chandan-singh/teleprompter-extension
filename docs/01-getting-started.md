# 01. Getting Started Guide

Get up and running with **Teleprompter** in less than two minutes.

---

## 1. Installation

### From the Chrome Web Store

1. Open the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore) listing for **Teleprompter**.
2. Click **Add to Chrome**.
3. Confirm by clicking **Add extension** in the browser dialog.
4. For quick access, click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions) in your Chrome toolbar and click the **Pin** icon next to Teleprompter.

---

## 2. Opening the Prompter Studio

Click the **Teleprompter** icon in your browser toolbar.

Unlike typical extension pop-ups that vanish when you click outside of them, Teleprompter opens in its own **full-screen browser tab**. This guarantees:

- The prompter never accidentally closes mid-presentation.
- You have the full width of your monitor or secondary teleprompter glass display.
- Browser extensions and notifications won't interrupt your reading flow.

---

## 3. Creating & Managing Scripts

### Writing a New Script

1. In the top navigation bar, click the **+ New Script** button.
2. Enter a descriptive title (e.g., `Product Launch Keynote` or `YouTube Intro`).
3. Type or paste your script content into the editor.
4. Scripts **auto-save automatically** to your local browser storage as you type.

### Importing Existing Files

Have a pre-written presentation? Click **Import** or drag and drop files onto the script list:

- **Microsoft Word (`.docx`)**: Preserves your paragraph breaks and structure.
- **Markdown (`.md`)**: Automatically loads headings and formatted text.
- **Plain Text (`.txt`)**: Clean UTF-8 text files.
- **Public URL**: Fetch public speech text or article content directly via URL.

### Tags, Pinning & Organization

- **Add Tags**: Add topic tags (e.g., `Meeting`, `Podcast`, `Pitch`) to quickly filter large script libraries.
- **Pin Favorites**: Click the pin icon next to any script to keep it anchored at the very top of your list.
- **Duplicate**: Use the duplicate button to create a rehearsal clone before making live edits.

---

## 4. Microphone Setup (For Voice-Sync Mode)

When you first select **Voice-Sync** mode or start a rehearsal, Chrome will display a permission prompt in the address bar:

> _"Teleprompter wants to: Use your microphone"_

1. Click **Allow**.
2. If you accidentally clicked "Block", click the lock/tune icon in the left side of Chrome's address bar, set **Microphone** to **Allow**, and reload the tab (`Ctrl+R` or `Cmd+R`).
3. You can verify your microphone connection anytime via the in-app **Help & Diagnostics** center (`F1` or Help button).

---

## 5. Testing in the Mini-Prompter Sandbox

Before stepping in front of your live audience, try the interactive sandbox:

1. Click the **Help & Docs** icon (`F1`) in the top navigation bar.
2. Select **Quick Start Tutorial**.
3. Scroll down to **Step 4 — Try the mini-prompter sandbox**.
4. Press `Space` to practice starting and pausing, use `↑`/`↓` to nudge the position, and speak aloud to see real-time word highlighting in action.

---

## 6. Launching Your Presentation

1. Select your desired script from the library.
2. Choose your operating mode from the dropdown:
   - **Voice-Sync** (Follows your voice)
   - **WPM Auto-Scroll** (Continuous automated roll)
   - **Paragraph** (Step-by-step click-through)
3. Set your target font size using the `+` / `-` buttons.
4. Click **Start** or press `Space` to begin!
