# 04. Camera & Display Setup Guide

Maintaining natural eye contact with your camera or audience is the #1 secret to a great presentation. Teleprompter includes specialized optical tools to ensure your eyes appear locked on the camera lens rather than scanning a screen.

---

## 1. Physical Window & Webcam Placement

To make your reading look completely natural:

1. **Position the Teleprompter window at the top of your screen**, centered directly beneath your physical webcam lens.
2. If using an external webcam on a mount, lower it so it rests as close as possible to the top edge of your browser window.
3. If using teleprompter beam-splitter glass (where a tablet or monitor reflects through a one-way mirror in front of a camera lens), ensure the window covers the reflection area.

---

## 2. Horizontal Reading Line Guide & Height Presets

The **Reading Line Guide** is a subtle horizontal indicator line that shows where your active reading gaze should stay.

### Quick Presets (Settings → Appearance or `[` / `]`)

- **Webcam Focus (20%)**: Positions the active text line high up in the top fifth of the screen, just millimeters below your webcam lens. This provides the most convincing direct eye contact for video calls, webinars, and YouTube videos.
- **Upper Third (33%)**: Traditional cinematography eye-line position. Balances direct gaze with comfortable peripheral vision of upcoming sentences.
- **Center (50%)**: Positions reading line in the vertical middle of the screen. Best when reading from stage confidence monitors or podiums set further back.

You can fine-tune the reading line height at any time using the keyboard shortcuts `[` (move up) and `]` (move down).

---

## 3. Distance-to-Font Calculator

Font size that is too small forces you to squint; font size that is too large forces rapid eye movement from left to right, making it obvious you are reading.

To calculate the mathematically optimal font size:

1. Open **Settings** → **Appearance & Sizing**.
2. Click **Calculate size by distance**.
3. Choose your viewing distance:
   - **Desk Setup (~50–70 cm / 2 ft)**: Laptop or desktop monitor. Suggested: `32px – 42px`.
   - **Standing Studio (~1.5–2.5 m / 5–8 ft)**: Video lighting setup with camera on tripod. Suggested: `52px – 68px`.
   - **Stage / Conference (~3–5 m / 10–16 ft)**: Floor wedge monitor or podium display. Suggested: `72px – 96px`.
4. Click **Apply Font Size** to save the recommendation.

---

## 4. Dark Mode vs. Light Mode

Configure in **Settings** → **Appearance**:

- **Dark Mode (Default & Recommended)**: Deep black background (`#0a0d14`) with high-contrast, off-white typography. Reduces pupil contraction, eliminates eye strain during long shoots, and avoids casting a bright white glare from the screen onto your face or glasses.
- **Light Mode**: High-contrast dark text on a clean, neutral background. Suitable for bright outdoor filming or well-lit conference rooms.

## 5. Script Annotations & Stage Cues

Teleprompter supports inline formatting tags to help you pace your delivery, remember to breathe, and add stage directions. These tags are parsed visually in the prompter and never read aloud:

| Annotation Syntax           | Visual Display                       | Purpose                                                            |
| :-------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `[pause]` or `[pause 2s]`   | Amber pause pill (⏸ 2s)              | Reminds you to take a breath or pause for dramatic effect          |
| `[look up]`                 | Teal eye pill (👁 Look up)            | Reminds you to break gaze and connect directly with audience       |
| `[slow]`                    | Blue pacing pill (🐢 Slow down)      | Prompt to slow down for complex technical explanations             |
| `[fast]`                    | Orange pacing pill (🐇 Pick up pace) | Prompt to accelerate tempo for energetic sections                  |
| `[breath]`                  | Cyan breathing pill (💨 Breath)      | Strategic breath mark                                              |
| `[emphasis]text[/emphasis]` | Bold amber highlight                 | Highlights key words or punchlines for vocal inflection            |
| `[stage: Smile and wave]`   | Grey italic cue card                 | Freeform director notes, slide cues, or physical movements         |
| `// your comment here`      | Completely hidden                    | Private notes or research citations stripped from the teleprompter |

## 6. The Automated "Look-Up" Cue

Even with optimal screen positioning, staring continuously at a prompter can make a speaker appear stiff.

Teleprompter includes an automated **Look-Up Cue** feature:

- Every _N_ paragraphs (default: every 3 paragraphs), a subtle pulse illuminates along the screen border.
- This acts as a gentle subconscious reminder to look up from the text, smile, or make direct gesture contact with your listeners.
- The frequency can be adjusted or turned off in **Settings** → **Prompter & Scrolling**.
